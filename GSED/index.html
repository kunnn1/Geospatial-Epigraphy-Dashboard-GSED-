The Monotheistic Inscriptions of Pre-Islamic Arabia

Every datable Jewish, Christian or ḥanīf inscription between roughly 400 to 630 CE, plotted onto the map of pre-Islamic Arabia. Huge thanks to Ilkka Lindstedt and his stellar work on pre-Islamic Arabian epigraphy for this idea, his own mapping of monotheist inscriptions in pre-Islamic Arabia (from his Muḥammad and His Followers in Context: The Religious Map of Late Antique Arabia) was the single most valuable source for this project.
